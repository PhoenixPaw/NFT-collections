Parvati #61 — Phoenix of Devotion and Power

Her visage and form are split into two luminous halves: the left, ruby and gold, radiates devotion and warmth; the right, sapphire and silver, embodies inner discipline and divine balance. 

Feathers cascade with HDR gradients that mirror this duality, while flowing robes carry sacred motifs. In her right hand she bears the trishula, the vow of resolve; from her left palm rises a living Flower of Life — a radiant sprout whose petals ascend through the colors of the chakras, from red to violet, signifying the path of awakening. 

She is the Phoenix of Devotion and Power — the harmonized heart and will that protect, uplift, and transform the world.
